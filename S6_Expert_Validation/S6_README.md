
# S6 Anonymized Expert-Validation Package

The panel comprised six industry experts (`E01`–`E06`) and six higher-vocational educators (`V01`–`V06`). The main workbook contains the item codebook, anonymized Round 1 and Round 2 rating matrices, bounded-anticipation ratings, and the revision log. The results workbook contains item-level and scale-level statistics, group comparisons, data-quality checks, Round 2 results, and the R1–R2 comparison. The final-item workbook records the final wording and decisions for all 42 candidate elements.

The archive contains no expert names, contact details, identity key, named invitation/consent files, or individual returned forms. Anonymous codes preserve the distinction between expert groups needed for the reported analysis.

To reproduce the expert statistics, run:

`python S6_expert_validation_analysis.py S6_expert_validation_data.xlsx`
